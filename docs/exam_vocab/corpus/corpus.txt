Many people believe that money is the most important thing in life. It seems impossible to live without money in today's society. With money, people can buy bigger houses, fancy cloths, expensive food, luxurious cars and travel all around the world. Money leads to a better life and ensure people's basic needs. Just like the old Chinese saying goes, money makes the mare go.

If a person really thinks of money as everything and pays his attention only to money, he may do anything that includes some evil things to get money.
